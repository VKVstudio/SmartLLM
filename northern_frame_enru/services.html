Operational Constraints & Safety

Do you handle all permits for filming in restricted Arctic zones?

Yes. We manage all permit applications, coordinate transport, and ensure crew safety compliance for remote locations.

How long does the pre-production phase take?

Pre-production planning, including script development and logistics coordination, is completed within 10 business days.

Is the footage available in both English and Russian?

Yes. All projects are delivered with bilingual narrative structures, ensuring accessibility for international and local audiences.

What happens if the weather conditions become unsafe during the shoot?

We prioritize crew safety above all. If conditions become unsafe, we pause filming and reschedule to ensure the integrity of the project and the safety of the team.

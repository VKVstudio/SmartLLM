Quality & Standards

Certifications

Every THERMOTECH boiler is manufactured under internationally recognized quality management systems and carries full certification for global markets.

ISO
ISO 9001:2015

Quality management system certification. Ensures consistent manufacturing processes, customer satisfaction, and continuous improvement across all production stages.

GOST
GOST R

Russian state standard certification for pressure equipment. Confirms compliance with Russian Federation technical regulations for boiler and pressure vessel safety.

CE
CE Marking

Conformité Européenne marking per Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU. Authorizes placement on the European Economic Area market with full traceability.

ASME
ASME U-Stamp

American Society of Mechanical Engineers certification for power boilers. Validates design, fabrication, and inspection per ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical and commercial questions about THERMOTECH boiler products, delivery, and support. Contact our engineering team for project-specific inquiries.

What is the typical delivery time for a THERMOTECH boiler?
Standard delivery is 8–12 weeks from confirmed order and deposit. Custom configurations may extend lead time by 2–4 weeks.
Can THERMOTECH boilers operate on alternative fuels?
Yes. All THERMOTECH units are designed for natural gas as the primary fuel and can be configured for dual-fuel operation (gas/oil) or biomass firing upon request.
What certifications do your boilers carry?
Every unit is manufactured under ISO 9001:2015 quality management and carries GOST R certification for the Russian market, plus CE marking for EU markets.
Do you provide on-site commissioning support?
Yes. Our commissioning team provides on-site installation supervision, functional testing, and operator training within the standard delivery scope.
What is the warranty coverage?
A standard 24-month warranty applies from the commissioning date, covering parts and labor for manufacturing defects. Extended warranty packages are available.
What is the minimum order quantity?
THERMOTECH manufactures units on a per-project basis. There is no minimum order quantity; each boiler is engineered to site-specific requirements.

Pressure-part strategy

Drum, tube bundles, headers, and supports are designed for fatigue margin, thermal expansion, and inspection access. Material traceability and welding documentation are maintained through fabrication.

Combustion and emissions

Burner selection, flue-gas recirculation, and furnace geometry are tuned for stable ignition, low excess air, and NOx control within the specified range.

Controls and safety

PLC logic handles burner management, flame safeguard, pressure and temperature limits, feedwater control, and alarm annunciation. DCS integration is available for plant-wide automation.

03 / FAQ

Frequently asked engineering questions

What output range do Thermotech boilers cover?

The standard product line covers 5 MW, 12 MW, and 25 MW thermal output. Custom variants can be engineered for adjacent duties when the required pressure, temperature, fuel, and site constraints are compatible with the base design.

Can the boilers operate on fuels other than natural gas?

Yes. The standard burners are configured for natural gas or light oil. Dual-fuel and alternative-fuel configurations can be specified with the appropriate burner, control, and safety package.

What automation and control systems are supplied?

Each boiler is delivered with PLC-based control, local HMI, safety interlocks, and standard communication interfaces. DCS integration, remote diagnostics, and advanced load-management packages are available on request.

Which codes and certifications are covered?

Design, fabrication, and testing can be executed to ISO, GOST, and applicable national pressure-equipment requirements. Documentation packages include material certificates, welding records, pressure-test reports, and conformity declarations.

What site information is needed for a quotation?

Please provide required duty, medium, pressure and temperature, fuel availability, ambient conditions, water quality, electrical supply, installation constraints, and any local regulatory requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Technical Q&A on ISO 3382, RT60, STI and structural decoupling.

What does ISO 3382 measure in a room?+
ISO 3382 specifies measurement methods for acoustic parameters of rooms, including reverberation time, early decay time, clarity and spatial impression, using omnidirectional source and omnidirectional microphone techniques.
How is RT60 calculated?+
RT60 is the time for the sound level to decay by 60 dB after the source stops. We derive it from room volume, total surface absorption coefficient and source level, following the Sabine equation and its modern weighted variants.
What is the Speech Transmission Index and why does it matter?+
The Speech Transmission Index (STI) is a numeric measure, from 0 to 1, of how intelligible speech is transmitted through a room. Values above 0.75 indicate good intelligibility, while values below 0.45 indicate poor intelligibility.
Why is structural decoupling important for vibration metrology?+
Structural decoupling isolates the metrology chamber from building-borne vibration and ground noise, so that laser Doppler vibrometry and anechoic measurements are not contaminated by ambient structural energy.
How do you calibrate a chamber before booking?+
Calibration follows accredited procedures: reference sound source verification, microphone linearity checks and laser vibrometer alignment. Calibration windows are booked through the form and confirmed by our metrology team.

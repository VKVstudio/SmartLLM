Frequently Asked Questions

What is AETHERIA BIO-ROBOTICS?

AETHERIA BIO-ROBOTICS is an autonomous environmental and precision agriculture drone platform.

Which platforms are available?

The AETHER-X4 Multispectral, AETHER-H8 Heavy Lidar, and AETHER-S2 Soil Probe platforms are available.

Can the calculator estimate battery swaps?

Yes. The flight-path coverage calculator estimates flight time, battery swaps, waypoint count, and coverage time.

How does the telemetry filter work?

The telemetry filter lets users isolate specific drone platforms and view related sensor, link, and payload metadata.

Is deployment inquiry validation client-side?

Yes. The deployment inquiry form uses client-side validation before submission.

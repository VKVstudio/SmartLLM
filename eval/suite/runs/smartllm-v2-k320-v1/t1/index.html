Boiler circuit — water, steam, and flue paths

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical lead time for a Thermotech boiler order?

Standard lead time is 14–18 weeks from order confirmation for hot-water models and 20–26 weeks for steam models, depending on configuration and fuel system.

Can Thermotech supply turnkey installation?

Yes. We deliver complete boiler houses including feedwater treatment, controls, stack, and commissioning, with supervision teams on site.

Do the boilers comply with ISO and GOST requirements?

Every unit is manufactured under ISO 9001:2015 quality management and certified for GOST-R and GOST 12.1.020 equipment safety requirements.

Which fuels are supported?

Natural gas, diesel, fuel oil, and biomass-derived fuels; dual-fuel burners are available on request.

What warranty and service coverage is included?

A 24-month warranty on pressure parts and burners, with optional service contracts covering annual inspections, spare parts, and remote diagnostics.

FAQ

5 common engineering questions
What fuel options are available for Thermotech boilers?
Thermotech boilers can be configured for natural gas, light oil, biogas, biomass, or waste-heat recovery depending on the model and site requirements.
What is the typical lead time for manufacturing?
Standard production lead time is 12 to 24 weeks after final engineering approval, depending on model, pressure class, and delivery scope.
Can Thermotech supply complete boiler-house packages?
Yes. We supply complete packages including burner, control panel, feedwater system, flue gas path, insulation, piping interfaces, and commissioning support.
How are emissions and combustion compliance handled?
Combustion settings are tuned for site-specific fuel quality and local emission limits. We provide burner documentation, test reports, and compliance support.
What maintenance access is provided?
Models include removable front covers, tube access points, inspection ports, and service clearances compatible with standard boiler-house maintenance.

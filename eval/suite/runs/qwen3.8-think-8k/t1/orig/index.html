Boiler circuit — TT-12000 reference design

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What output range do THERMOTECH boilers cover?
THERMOTECH manufactures packaged hot-water and steam boilers from 5 MW to 25 MW. The TT-5000 covers 5 MW hot-water duty, TT-12000 covers 12 MW steam, and TT-25000 covers 25 MW multi-fuel steam. Custom configurations outside this range are available on engineering request.
Which fuels are supported?
Standard burners are configured for natural gas and light diesel oil. The TT-25000 multi-fuel platform accepts heavy fuel oil, associated petroleum gas, and biomass-derived syngas with a dedicated burner management system and flame monitoring.
How long is the lead time from order to commissioning?
Typical lead time is 16–22 weeks for the TT-5000, 22–28 weeks for TT-12000, and 32–40 weeks for TT-25000, including factory hydrostatic and functional testing. On-site commissioning by THERMOTECH engineers adds 2–4 weeks.
Are the boilers certified for the EU and CIS markets?
Yes. All units carry CE marking under PED 2014/68/EU with conformity to EN 12953, and GOST R certification for CIS deployment. ASME S/U stamping is available on request for North-American projects.
What warranty and service coverage is included?
Every boiler includes a 24-month warranty from commissioning, extendable to 48 months with a service contract. THERMOTECH operates 24/7 remote diagnostics and a field-service network with a guaranteed 24-hour on-site response in covered regions.
